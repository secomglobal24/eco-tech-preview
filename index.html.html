Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about e-waste recycling and our services.

Electronic waste (e-waste) refers to discarded electronic devices and equipment such as computers, smartphones, televisions, and other electronic appliances that have reached the end of their useful life. These items contain valuable materials that can be recovered, as well as potentially hazardous components that require proper handling and disposal.

Data destruction is crucial because electronic devices often contain sensitive personal or business information. Without proper data wiping or physical destruction, this information can be recovered and potentially misused, leading to privacy breaches, identity theft, or corporate espionage. Our certified data destruction services ensure that all data is completely and irreversibly removed before recycling.

Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) is a policy approach that places the responsibility for the entire lifecycle of a product, including its end-of-life disposal and recycling, on the manufacturer or producer. In India, EPR regulations require electronics manufacturers and importers to collect and responsibly recycle a certain percentage of the products they sell. Tech Green Industries helps companies meet these regulatory requirements through our authorized EPR compliance services.

Getting started is simple. Contact us through our website, email, or phone to discuss your e-waste recycling needs. Our team will assess your requirements, provide a customized solution, and guide you through the entire process from collection to final disposal. We handle all the logistics, documentation, and compliance reporting, making the process seamless for you.
